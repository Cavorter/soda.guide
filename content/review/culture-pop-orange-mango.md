Medium sweet. This one looks and feels a lot like deja vu, but it's not. The [previous review for an Orange Mango flavor from Culture Pop included chili](/review/culture-pop-orange-mango-chili/), while this one does not! Plus the color of the can is much more orange, so it's clearly a new soda. :-) This time out the mango settles in for the long haul and barely lets any other flavors come through. This is a hint of orange in the middle, but oddly slightly more of somewhat woody coconut. The more I look for that woody flavor, the more I find it. Maybe don't try as hard as I am and it'll be much better. Medium long, but very pleasant finish actually still has a hint of capsaicin which makes me wonder if this actually _is_ the same flavor but with a new label? Hard to say at this point. Ok
