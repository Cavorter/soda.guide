Very sweet. Huge vanilla right off the start nearly completely obscures the classic citrus underneath. I suppose in that way this is mostly as advertised: it is certainly a fairly decent cream soda. The vanilla flavor is pretty good. Big, but not offensive. The finish is also better than I usually expect while "doin' the Dew", if a touch long. Probably the only problem I really have with it is the attempt to cash in on the "dirty" soda craze. Other than being a little cloudy to look at, there isn't anything that makes this anything other than a fairly cynical take on the trend. Cream soda fans and Mountain Dew fans will certainly be happy. For me: Ok.
