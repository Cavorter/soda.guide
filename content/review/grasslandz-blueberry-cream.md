Very sweet. Slightly tangy berry start has a touch more acid than expected, but a very pleasant level. I usually expect a "cream" soda to have a much more muted sharpness, but not with this one. The elderberry and vanilla rise in the middle as the blueberry fades and grow slightly into the medium length finish where a true blueberry fruit flavor subtley takes residence. Not quite what thought I was going to get, but in all the right ways. Recommended

Purchased from [Northeast Minneapolis Farmers Market](https://www.northeastmarket.org/)
