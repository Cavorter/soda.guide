Very sweet. A really good example of the classic "Red Pop" and/or a fairly decent, if incredibly artificial, vanilla soda. Strong hints of good quality circus peanut candy. Still somewhat cloying in sweetness and nearly perfume levels of artificial vanilla, but makes up for it a bit in nostalgia for the right drinker. Ok

Purchased at [Tim and Tom's Speedy Market](https://www.timandtomsspeedymarket.com/)
