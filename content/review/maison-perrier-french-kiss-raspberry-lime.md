Quite sweet. The unmistakable tang of sucralose is pretty immediate amid the distinct raspberry and lime flavors. Slightly sharp, but blunted fairly well by the fructose/sucralose combination that pervades. The basic flavor doesn't really go anywhere. Fruit tails off about midway through the extended finish. An experience bookended on both ends by sucralose. Ok.

Purchased at [Tim and Tom's Speedy Market](https://www.timandtomsspeedymarket.com/).
